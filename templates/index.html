📖 How to Play YumRush

🎯 Goal & Objective

Control the chef's basket along the kitchen counter to catch falling food before it hits the floor. Survive the full 120 seconds and achieve the highest possible score!

❤️ Lives & ⏱️ Timer

  • You start with 3 Lives (❤️ ❤️ ❤️).
  • Every food item that reaches the bottom costs 1 Life.
  • If you run out of lives, the game ends immediately!
  • The clock runs for exactly 2:00 (120 seconds). Score as much as you can before time expires.

🍎 Food Menu & Points

🍎 Apple +10 pts
🍟 Fries +15 pts
🍔 Burger +20 pts
🍕 Pizza +25 pts
🌮 Taco +30 pts
🧁 Chef Special +50 pts (Bonus!)

⚡ Difficulty & Levels

Reaching score milestones advances your level! As levels increase, food falls faster and spawns with higher frequency.

🕹️ Controls

Keyboard: ⬅️ / ➡️ Arrow Keys or A / D

Mouse / Touch: Move or drag your cursor/finger across the screen to steer the basket.

Pause: Press Esc or the Pause button anytime.
